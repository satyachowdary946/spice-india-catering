Spice India Catering Build 14.3 Hotfix

Changes:
- Removed the separate Confirmed Order Controls / Deposit & Kitchen card from order detail.
- Deposit status is now Paid / Part Paid / Not Paid and opens in a same-page modal from Dashboard, Orders, and Order Finance.
- Dashboard and Orders show Deposit, Kitchen, and payment-state quick indicators for confirmed/completed orders.
- Kitchen quick status links to the existing Kitchen Sheet & Sharing section; sending remains only there and only for Confirmed orders.
- Email quote action becomes a full green ticked action after successful backend send.
- WhatsApp quote action becomes a full green ticked action after the WhatsApp share is opened/prepared; separate Mark Sent button removed.
- Confirmed/completed order detail has Paid / Part Paid / Not Paid transaction shortcuts that open the order Transactions payments section.
- Minimum customer booking lead time changed to next calendar day.
- Existing Build 14.2 behavior and data compatibility preserved.
